ABOUT ME

I am currently employed at Marquette University as a Doctoral Candidate, aiming to complete my Doctoral Degree by 2025. My primary goal is to excel as an educator and researcher, focusing on educational research. I integrate educational theory and design to optimize learning outcomes for students. My work involves designing sophisticated systems to enhance the quality of computer science education. Notably, systems I have co-designed have been accessed well over 200,000 times, having a direct impact on hundreds of students. When time allows, my interests include the impact of AI on education, embedded systems, compilers, mobile application development, and video game design.

Projects

TA-BOT

TA-Bot is one of the world's most dynamic code assessment systems. It supports 80+ languages including single, multiple file, and complex assessment of student-built Operating Systems and Compilers in a secure code execution sandbox. The System has assessed over 100,000 total files across three years, in a multitude of courses.

Embedded Xinu

Embedded Xinu is an ongoing research and implementation project in the area of Operating Systems and Embedded Systems. Its original goal was to re-implement and port the Xinu Operating System to several embedded MIPS platforms. I helped port and build out the RISCV port of Embedded Xinu which has been used in two semesters of Operating Systems.
